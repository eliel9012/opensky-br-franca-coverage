- Era B full: 137 elapsed days, 36,535,422 positions, 4,925 aircraft.
- Common fleet: 2,234 aircraft; pooled mean range 188.5 to 217.9 km (+15.6%).

- Three declared ducting windows; maximum observed range 786.3 km.
- Source scripts, canonical macro data, aggregate reports and figure PDFs included.
- A0 portrait poster uses the selected navy sidebar, the original plots and a sky-blue FRC receiver star.
- QR uses the existing all-versions DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20192179.

Raw traces, aircraft-index caches and the unfinalized short paper are excluded.
